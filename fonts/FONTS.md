# Fonts

No font is bundled. `HorizontalDrawer` (and the config's `font_file`) needs a
path to a TrueType/OpenType file you supply.

Drop the files anywhere and point `font_file` at them (e.g.
`fonts/AnimeAce2.ttf` or an absolute path).

## Known-good comic faces

| font | where | license |
|---|---|---|
| Anime Ace 2.0 | blambot.com | free for non-commercial; no redistribution |
| Blambot Classic | blambot.com | free for non-commercial; no redistribution |
| Komika | dafont.com | free |
| CC Wild Words | commercial (Comicraft) | paid |
